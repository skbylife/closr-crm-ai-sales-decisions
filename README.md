## English

### Read the customer. See the deal. Decide the next move.

Closr is an open-source, self-hosted CRM being prepared for sales teams that need more than a record of what already happened.

Customer signals are scattered across emails, meetings, messages, CRM notes, and follow-ups. Closr brings those signals into one account view so a team can review what changed, what is still unconfirmed, and what to do next.

### What Closr is built to help with

- **Core CRM** — manage accounts, contacts, opportunities, activities, and follow-ups.
- **Customer signals** — bring communication and account context into the sales conversation.
- **Sales decisions** — surface risks, missing information, stakeholder changes, and priority actions for review.
- **Team execution** — turn account context into an owner, a next step, and a question to validate.
- **Learning over time** — preserve account history so teams can revisit what changed and why.

### A simple sales loop

```text
Customer signals → account context → team review → next move → outcome → learning
```

Closr is designed to make that loop visible and usable without turning CRM work into a maze.

### Open-source status

This public repository is intentionally **README-only** while the first source release is prepared.

The goal is a genuinely useful project for small and growing teams to self-host, use, improve, and contribute to—not a restricted demo that requires another product to be useful.

### Product principles

- Customer evidence should remain visible alongside AI-assisted suggestions.
- A suggested interpretation is a hypothesis to review, not an unquestionable fact.
- People remain responsible for customer communication and business decisions.
- The open-source CRM should stand on its own for everyday team use.

### Foundation Gen

Foundation Gen is a separate future commercial product. It is not required to use Closr and is not a gated feature of the open-source CRM.

### Follow the project

Watch this repository for the first public source release, self-hosting documentation, and contribution guidelines.

## 中文

### 读懂客户，看清局势，决定下一步。

Closr 是一个正在准备发布的开源、自部署 CRM，面向需要的不只是“记录已经发生了什么”的销售团队。

客户信号散落在邮件、会议、消息、CRM 备注和跟进记录中。Closr 将这些信号汇聚到同一个账户视图，让团队共同复盘：什么发生了变化、什么仍待确认、下一步应该做什么。

### Closr 希望解决什么

- **基础 CRM** —— 管理账户、联系人、商机、活动与跟进。
- **客户信号** —— 将沟通内容和账户上下文带回销售讨论。
- **销售判断** —— 提示值得复核的风险、信息缺口、角色变化和优先动作。
- **团队推进** —— 将账户上下文转化为负责人、下一步动作和待验证的问题。
- **持续学习** —— 保存账户历史，让团队能够复盘变化及其原因。

### 一个简单的销售闭环

```text
客户信号 → 账户上下文 → 团队复盘 → 下一步行动 → 结果 → 学习
```

Closr 希望让这个闭环可见、可用，而不是把 CRM 变成复杂的填写负担。

### 开源状态

本公开仓库目前有意保持为 **README-only**，第一版源代码正在准备公开发布。

目标是让中小团队可以自部署、真实使用、持续改进并参与贡献；不是一个必须购买其他产品才有价值的受限演示版。

### 产品原则

- 客户证据应与 AI 辅助建议一起可见。
- AI 给出的解读是需要复核的假设，不是不可质疑的事实。
- 客户沟通和业务判断始终由人负责。
- 开源 CRM 应独立满足团队的日常使用。

### Foundation Gen

Foundation Gen 是未来独立推出的商业产品。使用 Closr 不需要 Foundation Gen；它也不是开源 CRM 中被锁住的功能。

### 关注项目

欢迎 Watch 本仓库，关注第一版开源代码、自部署文档和贡献指南。
